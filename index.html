Common questions

Free covers the volume calculator, test logging with instant Low/OK/High verdicts, the last 7 days of history, and liquid chlorine dosing for one chlorine pool.

The one-time PoolDose Pro purchase adds every other chemical (acids, Cal-Hypo, dichlor, trichlor, baking soda, calcium chloride, CYA, salt, bromine), salt water and bromine pools, unlimited history and trend charts, reminders, and multiple pools. It is a single purchase, not a subscription, and it does not expire.

Open Settings → View PoolDose Pro → Restore purchase while signed in with the same Apple ID you bought it with. The unlock is tied to the Apple ID, not the device, so it carries to a new phone.

Where do the amounts come from?

Linear parameters (chlorine, alkalinity, calcium, CYA, salt, bromine) are pure stoichiometry from your water volume and the product's active-ingredient percentage. pH uses a carbonate-plus-cyanurate buffer model, pinned in tests against the published dosing tables.

Every result carries a per-addition safety cap, a split-dose plan when the amount is large, and a retest interval. Amounts round toward under-dosing rather than over-dosing.

Why doesn't it read my test strip with the camera?

Because camera strip reading is unreliable in real conditions — lighting, shade and camera differences move the colors, and that is the single biggest complaint against the apps that do it. PoolDose shows the color swatches on screen instead: you hold your strip next to them and tap the closest match, or type the number. People are good at comparing two colors side by side; putting a camera in between breaks it.

Does it work without a signal?

Yes — everything runs on your device. There is no account, no sign-in, and no server. The app never needs a connection except when the App Store handles your purchase.

Which units does it use?

Both. Gallons or liters for volume, feet or meters for pool dimensions, oz/lb or g/mL for doses, °F or °C for water temperature. Switch the whole app in Settings → Units.

Safety

Read this before you add anything

PoolDose gives general guidance based on standard pool chemistry. It is not a substitute for the instructions on your chemical product labels.

  • If the label and the app disagree, follow the label.
  • Never mix chemicals with each other — especially chlorine products with acid.
  • Add chemicals to water, never water to chemicals.
  • Store them away from children and pets.
  • When in doubt, add less, circulate, and retest before adding more.
