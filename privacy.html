PoolDose Privacy Policy

PoolDose collects nothing. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no server to send anything to.

Last updated: August 2026

What we collect

Nothing. PoolDose has no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising identifiers and no third-party SDKs that gather data. The developer never receives your pool data, your device identifiers or your location.

Where your data lives

Your pools, test results, dose logs and reminders are stored in a database inside the app on your device. They are included in your own iPhone or iCloud device backup, which only you control. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Purchases

The one-time Pro unlock is processed by Apple through the App Store. PoolDose only asks Apple whether this Apple ID owns the unlock. Payment details are handled by Apple and are never visible to the app or the developer. See Apple's privacy policy for how Apple handles purchases.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally on your device. PoolDose has no push server and no push token — nothing about your reminders leaves your iPhone.

Children

PoolDose is a general-audience utility rated 4+. It does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes & contact

If this policy ever changes, the updated text ships with the app and appears on this page with a new date.
