How the math works

Raising 10,000 gallons by 1 ppm takes about 37.9 g of available chlorine. The calculator scales that by your volume and the ppm gap, then divides by the strength of the product you picked. Doses are rounded down — adding a little less and retesting beats overshooting.

Amounts above the safe single-addition limit are split into stages, with a retest between each. That limit is per 10,000 gallons and depends on the product.
